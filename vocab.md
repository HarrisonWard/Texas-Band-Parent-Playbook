# The Band Dictionary

*Every word the WISK assumes you already know. Plain definitions, FloMo flavor included.*

The starter guide has the ten-word survival glossary. This is the full decoder ring: skim it once in August and suddenly the emails make sense.

## People you will hear about

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Directors | The faculty. They run the program, teach the classes, and answer the hard questions. |
| Techs / staff | Hired specialists who coach one thing: brass tech, guard tech, percussion tech. Not faculty, absolutely part of the machine. |
| Clinician | An outside expert brought in to sharpen the show. When an unfamiliar name shows up on the calendar next to a stadium rehearsal, that's a clinician day, and it matters. |
| Mr. Spaeth | FloMo's recurring example of the above. When the WISK says "Mr. Spaeth here," expect long stadium nights and a noticeably better show afterward. |
| Drum major | The student conductor on the podium. There are usually several. Yes, it's a huge honor. |
| Section leader | The student in charge of one instrument's crew. Your kid's first boss. |
| Liaison | The parent volunteer who owns one slice of the program: drumline liaison, guard liaison. They know everything about their slice. |
| Boosters / the board | The parent nonprofit that funds and staffs everything the district doesn't. Budget like a small company, salary of zero. |

## The ensembles, decoded for FMHS

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Competition Band | The varsity marching band, around 200 members, the one at contests. Placement announced after evening rehearsals in week one (results post in Canvas). |
| Jaguar Band | The JV marching band. Same music, lighter travel, own rehearsal track. |
| Wind Symphony | The top concert band. This is the group performing at Midwest Clinic. |
| Symphonic, Concert, Varsity bands | The rest of the concert band ladder, by audition, spring semester's main event. |
| Battery | The drumline that marches: snares, tenors, basses. |
| Pit / front ensemble | The percussion parked at the front sideline: marimbas, vibes, synths, the big toys. They don't march, they haul. |
| Color guard | Flags, rifles, sabres, dance. Scored inside the band's score in fall, own circuit in winter. |
| Weapon line | The guard members who spin rifle and sabre. Advanced equipment, big deal, congratulate accordingly. |
| Winter guard | Guard's own indoor competitive season, roughly December through April, NTCA locally and WGI nationally. Separate fee, separate calendar. |
| Winter percussion | Same idea for the drumline, indoor season in the spring semester. |

## Events that will appear on your calendar

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Summer band / band camp | The late-July-through-August grind that builds the show. See the starter guide for survival. |
| Sectionals | Rehearsal for just one instrument group. Usually the afternoon block. |
| Masterclass | A visiting expert teaches one section during class time (woodwind masterclass, brass masterclass, dance masterclass). Free coaching from serious players. |
| Stadium rehearsal | Evening full-band rehearsal in the actual stadium, usually 6 to 9pm. The show gets real here. |
| Run-through / full run | Performing the whole show top to bottom without stopping. What everything else builds toward. |
| Pass-offs | Graded playing checkoffs on assigned music, tracked on a sheet each nine weeks. A quarter of the band grade. |
| Challenge | The process for a Jaguar Band member to evaluate into the Competition Band on marching and playing skills. The door is always open. |
| The itinerary | The minute-by-minute schedule for the week, distributed every Monday during marching season. The WISK's more detailed sibling. |
| Parent Preview | The first public performance of the show, parents invited, usually mid-August. Bring tissues, it sneaks up on you. |
| March-a-Thon | All-day fundraiser where the band marches and plays around town collecting donations. FloMo's is early September, 9am to 3pm. |
| Meet the Jags | FMHS's community pep rally in week one of school. All members perform. |
| Cluster pep rally | A rally for the FMHS feeder-school family (the "cluster"). Band plays, younger kids get recruited by osmosis. |
| FM9 | The ninth grade campus. Freshman band members split time between FM9 events and the main campus program. |
| DCI watch party | The band gathers to watch Drum Corps International finals on a big screen. DCI is the professional-adjacent summer marching circuit, and it's where half the staff's ideas come from. |
| Inspection | The pre-departure check before games and contests: uniform complete, instrument ready, kid presentable. Miss inspection, miss the bus. |
| Prelims / finals | Contest format: perform in the morning, wait, perform again at night if you advance. |
| Region, Area, State | The UIL playoff ladder. Region in October, Area next, State at the Alamodome in November. |
| Midwest Clinic | The band world's biggest conference, held in Chicago every December. Being invited to perform is a national-level honor for a concert band. FMHS Wind Symphony is going. Yes, that's a very big deal. |

## Gear your kid will suddenly own

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Dot | Your kid's exact coordinate on the field for one moment of the show. |
| Drill / sets | The full choreography, learned as numbered pages of dots. |
| Dot book | The little notebook (or app) with every dot in it. Losing it is a genuine crisis. |
| Football uniform | The game-night marching uniform, issued at registration. Worn for band photos too. |
| Bibbers | The overall-style uniform pants under the jacket. |
| Shako and plume | The tall uniform hat and the feather that rides in it. The plume box is sacred, do not touch. |
| Gauntlets | The wrist pieces. Perpetually almost lost. |
| Swoosh shirt | FMHS's navy spirit shirt, the default "look uniform without the uniform" outfit for pep rallies and appearances. |
| Show shirt | The annual t-shirt themed to this year's show. Wear it to contests, wash it Thursday nights. |
| Dinkles | Band shoes, technically a brand name that went generic. FMHS is in white shoes this year, hence the shoe forms. |
| Flag, rifle, sabre | Guard equipment, in ascending order of "gets thrown terrifyingly high." The rifle is wood and plastic, nothing fires. |
| Props | The scenery the band hauls on and off the field. Built, moved, and repaired by parent volunteers. |
| The trailer | The semi or box truck that moves instruments and props. Watching it get packed is watching a religion. |
| Stand tunes | The short songs the band plays in the bleachers all game. You will have all of them memorized by October. |

## Alphabet soup

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| UIL | University Interscholastic League, the Texas school activities league. Runs the Region, Area, State marching ladder, concert evaluations, and the 8-hour rule. |
| BOA | Bands of America, the national contest circuit. Regionals across Texas, San Antonio Super Regional at the Alamodome, Grand Nationals in Indianapolis. |
| WGI | Winter Guard International, the national winter guard and percussion circuit. Season ends in Dayton, Ohio every April. |
| NTCA | North Texas Colorguard Association, the DFW winter guard circuit where FloMo guards compete locally. |
| TCGC | Texas Color Guard Circuit, the equivalent circuit for much of the rest of Texas. |
| DCI | Drum Corps International, the elite summer marching circuit for age 16 to 22 performers. The big leagues your kid's techs probably marched in. |
| WISK | What I Should Know, the band's Sunday email through Presto. The official word. Condensed weekly in [the digest](wisk-digest-2026.md). |
| VOLY | LISD's volunteer portal. Annual background check, two weeks to clear, required before you can help. |

## FloMo traditions you will encounter

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| AMA | Ask Me Anything: summer Thursdays, board members in the band parking lot for the last hour of practice, answering any question. The officially sanctioned place to feel new. |
| Fruit break | During camp, each section brings assigned fruit on its assigned day and volunteers cut and serve it at morning break. Guard brings the apples on Saturday. |
| The WISK's 3-week outlook | The rolling calendar at the bottom of every WISK. Read it before scheduling anything, including dentist appointments and weddings. |
| Boo Buckets | A guard parent tradition: treat buckets for the kids around Halloween, funded by parent Venmo chip-ins. |
| Memory book | The end-of-year photo book built from the season's SmugMug galleries, with a student team helping collect shots. |
| Events named after food | Booster feed-the-band operations rotate through the season. If a food-named event appears in the WISK, volunteers and crockpots are about to be requested. |

Missing a word? Open an issue, or ask the nearest veteran parent, who will answer in three more words you don't know yet.
