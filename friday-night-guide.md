# The Friday Night Guide

*Football games, from call time to the midnight pickup lane*

Contests get the glory, but Friday nights are the actual job: ten of them this fall, some on Thursdays, every one ending with a kid climbing into your car past bedtime smelling like turf and brass. Here is how the night works.

## The kid's night

1. School ends. Home games: report to the band hall for call time. Away games: dismissal straight to inspection, then buses. There is no time to go home, which is why the meal form exists.
2. Inspection, in the Auditorium or Cafeteria, time listed on the weekly itinerary that comes out every Monday. Full uniform, tidy locker, instrument ready. The handbook's banned list is absolute: no makeup, no nail polish, no jewelry, no watches, long hair tied up and tucked in the hat. Fail inspection and you lose stands seating privileges. Per the handbook, the performance officially begins the moment they leave the inspection room and ends when they're back in the band hall after the game.
3. Pregame: the band marches in, plays the fight song and the anthem, sets the tone for the whole stadium.
4. First half: stand tunes, cheers, choreography with the student section, more playing than sitting. Assigned seats, full uniform, written parts only, and the handbook's finest sentence: "No spontaneous jam sessions."
5. Halftime: the show. Seven-ish minutes for which everyone did everything.
6. Third quarter: the traditional break. Kids get water and concessions and fifteen minutes of being teenagers.
7. Fourth quarter: back in the stands until the end. The band stays the whole game, always, both directions on the scoreboard.
8. Postgame: the band lines up on the track and returns to the band hall as a group, then unloads, then hangs the uniform correctly (the handbook is serious about this), then your car. Home games end near 11. Away games end past midnight. First period still exists tomorrow.

## Your night

- Buy tickets ahead on the district's system, home games through the [LISD box office](https://events.hometownticketing.com/boxoffice/lewisvilleisd), away games through the host district (all ten games mapped with addresses and ticket links on the [Calendars page](calendars.md)).
- Clear bag, cashless concessions, the usual stadium rules.
- Sit where you can see the band, not just the field. Watching your kid do stand tunes for two hours is half the entertainment.
- At halftime, put the phone down for one run-through and just watch. Then film Saturday's, there will be plenty.
- Do not extract your kid mid-game to say hi. The band operates as a unit from call time to dismissal.
- Pickup: follow the band's posted pickup procedures, park where directed, and bring patience. Four hundred kids and one parking lot resolve at their own speed. A snack in the car makes you a legend.

## Nights that are different

- **Thursday games**: three this season (Aug 27, Sep 3, Nov 5). Same night, school night, plan the morning after with mercy.
- **Senior night**: the last home game. Seniors and their parents get announced on the field before the game. Flowers happen, tears happen, underclassman parents take notes because it arrives faster than advertised.
- **Homecoming**: louder, longer, more mums than physics should allow. The band plays through all of it.
- **Playoffs**: if football keeps winning, Fridays keep going into December, at neutral sites announced week by week. The band goes where the team goes, and nobody complains because playoff games are electric.
- **Weather**: games play through rain. Lightning forces delays, sometimes long ones, and the band shelters with everyone else. The night ends when it ends.

## The quiet truth

Friday nights are where band stops being an activity and becomes the social fabric of your kid's high school life. The stands are where the sections bond, the inside jokes are born, and the freshmen become people. You are just the ride home. It is a great job.
