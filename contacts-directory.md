## The escalation ladder, so you use it in the right order

1. Check the WISK and the app (Presto for band, BAND for guard). It's answered there more often than not.
2. Ask the parent Facebook group. Someone hit your exact problem last year.
3. Ask the relevant liaison.
4. Email the role address from the table above.
5. Email a director. They answer, they're just also teaching 430 kids, so give it a day or two before a follow-up.

One etiquette rule carries this whole page: email, don't DM. Staff and booster officers live in their inboxes, not their Facebook messages.

## The AMA, the boosters' best onboarding trick

Summer Thursdays, the boosters run Ask Me Anything: board members and veteran parents post up in the band parking lot for the last hour of evening practice and take any question, no matter how new-parent it feels. Their words: band is a lot, you are not alone, we have all been there. Show up once with your dumbest questions and leave with a section parent's cell number and half this playbook confirmed in person.
