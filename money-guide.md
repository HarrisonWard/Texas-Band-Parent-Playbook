# The Money Guide

*What band actually costs, and how families actually pay for it*

Nobody hides the band fee. Everything else arrives one surprise at a time. This is the whole map, with Flower Mound's real numbers where we have them.

## The bill you can see

The FMHS band fee for 2026-27 is **$700, with a $250 deposit**, collected through JotForm at registration. It covers roughly what fees cover everywhere: game and contest meals, show shirts, entry fees, and the small army of instructors. Guard fees run their own track with costuming in the mix.

Payment plans are standard, spread across the fall. Nobody at the table blinks at installments. They blink at surprises, so get the deposit in on time.

## The bills you cannot see yet

| Item | Ballpark | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Marching shoes | $30 to $60 | White this year at FMHS, confirm or decline on the shoe form |
| Gloves, socks, under-uniform gear | $20 to $40 | Sections specify, kids lose gloves like it's a sport |
| School instrument fee | $100 a year | For school-owned horns, separate check to FMHS Band, covers routine maintenance. Damage beyond normal use bills separately. |
| Reeds, oil, sticks, heads | $10 to $30 a month | Woodwind parents know the reed economy is a racket |
| Private lessons | $24 per half hour, $48 per hour | Handbook rates. Strongly encouraged, and need-based lesson scholarships exist: meet privately with Mr. Biskup. |
| Solo and Ensemble | entry fee plus accompanist fee | Early March, required for winds and percussion, the accompanist covers two rehearsals and the performance |
| Uniform replacement, if lost | jacket $240, pants $133, shako $60 | Guard the garment bag ($12) like jewelry. Hangers are $5 and somehow always missing. |
| Away game meals | set by the program | Buy through the meal form or send food, the form is required either way |
| Merch | whatever you allow | The Square store is good and it knows it |
| Tea stops and spending money | $10 to $20 a week | The reloadable card from the starter guide earns its keep |
| Contest tickets for you | $10 to $40 per person per event | Football gates plus UIL and BOA admission adds up across a fall |
| FloMarching subscription | about $30 a month in season | Only if you want streams of the events you miss |

## The seasonal spikes

- **Winter guard and winter percussion**: separate seasons, separate fees, costume included. If your kid's guard makes the WGI trip, add travel to Dayton in April.
- **Trip years**: when the band travels big (a bowl game, a parade, a far contest), add $500 to $2,000, announced far ahead with its own payment plan.
- **Weapon kids who fly**: the long gun case with real locks, one-time, so the rifle can vacation with you.
- **Senior year**: banquet, memory book, senior night flowers, and every "last one" you didn't budget tears for.

## How families actually cover it

- **Payment plans**: standard, monthly, ask at registration.
- **Fundraising with credits**: some fundraisers credit your student's account directly. Mulch sales, recycling drives (the drumline's last one raised $3,141), March-a-Thon pledges. Treat them as tuition offsets, not bake sales.
- **The quiet path**: every big program has a discreet hardship process. One email to the directors, one sentence: "we need some help this season." It has been done a hundred times before you and nobody will ever know. A kid has never been marched out of a Texas band over money quietly asked about.
- **Booster donations are tax friendly**: the FMHS Band Boosters are a 501(c)(3), so direct donations may be deductible. Ask your tax person, not us.

## The honest total

For a wind player at FMHS in a normal year: fee plus gear plus tickets plus tea lands most families somewhere between $1,000 and $1,500 across the school year. Add winter guard and it climbs. Add a trip year and it climbs again. Compare it to a season of club sports and it suddenly looks reasonable, which is the most Texas sentence in this repo.
