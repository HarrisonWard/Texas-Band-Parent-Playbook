- The staff and boosters have done every big trip and big stage before. When they say trust the process, they have receipts.
- Expectations are real. The program's floor is high because its ceiling is.
- The younger siblings watching in the stands are watching a dynasty in progress. Cluster kids grow up wanting the swoosh shirt.
