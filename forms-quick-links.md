# Forms and Quick Links

*The fridge page. Every form you'll be asked for and every link you'll actually use.*

## The forms, and when they bite

| Form | When | Where it lives |
|------|------|----------------|
| UIL physical | Before camp, new for every student this season | Upload via the band's form, questions to willardt@lisd.net. District athletics side runs through [Rank One](https://lewisvilleisd.rankone.com/) |
| Medical history form | Every year, signed by parent and student | Comes with registration |
| Online registration plus deposit | July, makeup nights in August | Password-protected page on [fmhsband.org](http://www.fmhsband.org/), password in the WISK |
| Away Game Meal Form | Due August 21, required for EVERY student | Link in the WISK, questions to activities@fmhsband.org |
| Dietary Restriction Form | Due August 21, also required for every student | Link in the WISK |
| Marching shoe reply form | August, everyone answers yes or no | JotForm link in the WISK, exchanges via shoes@fmhsband.org |
| Band photo order | Early August, discount deadline is real, no makeup photo day | JotForm link and discount code in the WISK |
| VOLY background check | Before you volunteer for anything, takes up to 2 weeks | LISD VOLY portal, link in the WISK |
| Volunteer signups | All season | SignUpGenius links in the WISK and parent groups |

The pattern worth learning: permanent forms live on the band site and Rank One, seasonal forms arrive as JotForm links in the WISK, and volunteer shifts run through SignUpGenius. When in doubt, search your email for WISK first.
