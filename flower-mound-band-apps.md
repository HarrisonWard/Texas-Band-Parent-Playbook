# Every App a Flower Mound Band Parent Actually Needs

*The FMHS band and guard digital stack, in one giant list*

The program runs on more platforms than most companies and nobody hands you the list. Here it is, pulled from the band site, the booster page, the LISD district pages, and three seasons of posts in the guard parents Facebook group, checked August 2026. Items marked with a * are common in the program but not posted anywhere official, so confirm with a veteran parent.

## The must-haves, set these up in week one

| Platform | What it is | What you will use it for |
|----------|------------|--------------------------|
| [Presto Assistant](https://app.presto-assistant.com) | The band's student management system | The roster of record: even the parent Facebook groups check you against it before letting you in. Also handles the calendar, forms, district instrument rental paperwork and fees, and the weekly email newsletter everyone calls the WISK. Replaced Charms around 2024, so when a veteran parent says "check Charms," they mean this. |
| [Skyward Family Access](https://www.lisd.net/our-district/all-departments/technology/support-services/skyward) | LISD's parent portal | Grades, attendance, annual enrollment |
| [RevTrak](https://lewisville.revtrak.net/Skyward-Fee-Payments/) | LISD's payment site, linked from Skyward | School-side fee payments |
| [Rank One](https://lewisvilleisd.rankone.com/) | LISD's forms portal | Physicals and medical history paperwork for UIL activities |
| VOLY | LISD's volunteer portal | Annual background check, required before helping at any band activity. Takes up to two weeks, so file it before you plan to say yes to anything. Link comes through the WISK. |
| Google Forms and Drive | The band's document layer | The UIL physical form gets submitted through a Google Form, and the band handbook lives on Drive. Links come from the band. |
| [BAND](https://band.us/) | Group communication app | The guard's official channel: liaisons answer questions there and uniform pickup notices go out through it. Guard families, download it day one. |
| Canvas | LISD's class platform | Mostly your kid's homework world, but band registration emails have landed there too |
| [fmhsband.org](http://www.fmhsband.org/) | The band website | Schedules, FAQ, band camp packing list, pickup procedures, booster info |
| Band Google Calendars: [one](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=84jp7ctks4behcmpes3ocdb1n4%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FChicago), [two](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=nulalpfh5qm4fjm4nq8bhe5df0%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FChicago), [three](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=1m5pjqaes2pd3g5b68l9hhpuig%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America%2FChicago) | The season, hour by hour | The band runs a General calendar plus one per group. Subscribe to General and your kid's groups in Google Calendar and every rehearsal, game, and contest lands on your phone automatically. |

## Money and merch

| Platform | What you will use it for |
|----------|--------------------------|
| [FMHS Band Store on Square](https://my-site-103087.square.site/) | The band merch. There is a lot and it is good. |

## The student layer, theirs not yours

| App | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| Ultimate Drill Book * | Their drill and dots, on their phone. When they stare at their screen at dinner, this might actually be homework. |
| Tonal Energy * | The tuner and metronome app every Texas band kid has. The smiley face means they are in tune. |
| GroupMe / Discord section chats * | Where real information travels 20 minutes before any official channel. You will never see it. Make peace. |

## The honest summary

Call it fifteen platforms, and none of them talk to each other. This is normal. Set up the must-haves in week one, follow the socials, join the parent groups, and remember the split: Presto and BAND carry the official word, the Facebook groups carry the parent word, and the truth usually needs both. When all else fails, president@fmhsband.org reaches an actual human.
