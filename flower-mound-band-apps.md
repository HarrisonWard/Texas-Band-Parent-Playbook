# Every App a Flower Mound Band Parent Actually Needs

*The FMHS band and guard digital stack, in one giant list*

The program runs on roughly a dozen platforms and nobody hands you the list. Here it is, pulled from the band site, the booster page, and the LISD district pages, checked August 2026. Items marked with a * are common in the program but not posted anywhere official, so confirm with a veteran parent.

## The must-haves, set these up in week one

| Platform | What it is | What you will use it for |
|----------|------------|--------------------------|
| [Presto Assistant](https://app.presto-assistant.com) | The band's student management system | Roster, calendar, official emails, forms, finances. If a veteran parent says "Charms," this is what replaced it. |
| [Skyward Family Access](https://www.lisd.net/our-district/all-departments/technology/support-services/skyward) | LISD's parent portal | Grades, attendance, annual enrollment |
| [RevTrak](https://lewisville.revtrak.net/Skyward-Fee-Payments/) | LISD's payment site, linked from Skyward | School-side fee payments |
| [Rank One](https://lewisvilleisd.rankone.com/) | LISD's forms portal | Physicals and medical history paperwork for UIL activities |
| Google Forms and Drive | The band's document layer | The UIL physical form gets submitted through a Google Form, and the band handbook lives on Drive. Links come from the band. |
| [BAND](https://band.us/) | Group communication app | The color guard runs on it: schedules, changes, chatter. Guard families, download it day one. |
| [fmhsband.org](http://www.fmhsband.org/) | The band website | Schedules, FAQ, band camp packing list, pickup procedures, booster info |

## The student layer, theirs not yours

| App | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| Ultimate Drill Book * | Their drill and dots, on their phone. When they stare at their screen at dinner, this might actually be homework. |
| Tonal Energy * | The tuner and metronome app every Texas band kid has. The smiley face means they are in tune. |
| GroupMe / Discord section chats * | Where real information travels 20 minutes before any official channel. You will never see it. Make peace. |

## The honest summary

Six platforms before the first downbeat, four more on contest weekends, and none of them talk to each other. This is normal. Set up the must-haves in week one, follow the socials, and find one veteran parent for everything that falls between the cracks.
