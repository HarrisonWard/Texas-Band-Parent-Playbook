# Texas Band Parent Playbook

Plain-English guides for parents new to Texas high school marching band and color guard.

No pep talks. No essays about the magic of the activity. Just what happens, what it costs, what the rules are, and how to help, with sources.

## Why this exists

Band programs are wonderful at teaching kids and terrible at explaining themselves to parents. The information exists, but it is scattered across handbooks, booster meetings, and veteran-parent folklore, some of which is out of date. This repo collects it in one place and cites sources.

## Corrections

Fees change, UIL rewrites rules, dates roll over every season. If something here is wrong or stale, open an issue or a pull request. House rules live in [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).
