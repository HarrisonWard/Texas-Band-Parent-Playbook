## Put them on your phone

**iPhone, Apple Calendar**

1. Settings, then Calendar, then Accounts. On newer iOS it is Settings, then Apps, then Calendar, then Calendar Accounts.
2. Add Account, then Other, then Add Subscribed Calendar.
3. Paste an ICS link from the table, tap Next, tap Save.
4. Repeat for each calendar you want. Done, it updates itself forever.

**Android, Google Calendar**

1. Open calendar.google.com in a browser. Desktop mode is easiest.
2. On the left, find Other calendars and tap the plus sign.
3. Choose Subscribe to calendar and paste the subscribe address from the table.
4. It shows up in the Google Calendar app on its own. If it hides, open the app settings, find the calendar, and turn on sync and visibility.

**Outlook**

1. Open the Outlook calendar, choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
2. Paste an ICS link, name it something like FM Band, and add it.
3. Classic desktop Outlook: File, Account Settings, Internet Calendars tab, New, paste the ICS link.

These links work because the band publishes the calendars publicly. If one ever demands a login, the band changed a setting, check the WISK for the current instructions.
