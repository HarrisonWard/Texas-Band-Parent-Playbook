# The Freshman Year Timeline

*The whole first year on one page, so nothing ambushes you*

Every month of a first band year at a program like Flower Mound's, with the FloMo anchors we know. Your dates will wobble by a week or two. The shape will not.

| Month | What happens | What it costs you |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| **July** | Physicals uploaded, registration and deposit, band camp starts the last week. The gallon jug era begins. | Deposit, physical copay, camp gear, one emotional grocery run |
| **August** | Camp goes full days, school starts mid-month, Parent Preview, band photos, first game in week three. Forms cascade: meals, dietary, shoes, VOLY. | Fee installments, shoes, tickets, the first HTeaO of hundreds |
| **September** | The rhythm locks in: weeknight rehearsals, Friday games, March-a-Thon, first contests. All-Region etudes quietly take over the practice room. | Game tickets, contest gas, pledge money |
| **October** | Peak season. Contests most Saturdays, UIL Region then Area, homecoming chaos, the show peaks. Sleep is a rumor. | Contest tickets, more gas, a stadium seat you wish you'd bought in August |
| **November** | The summit: BOA San Antonio Super Regional, UIL State the next week, both at the Alamodome. Marching ends. Winter guard and percussion auditions. Concert band seating begins. All-Region auditions. | San Antonio hotel, State tickets, winter season deposit, audition fees |
| **December** | Winter ensembles start building. Holiday concert. Wind Symphony's Midwest Clinic trip. The first quiet Friday feels wrong. | Concert attire if needed, winter fees begin |
| **January** | Winter guard and percussion seasons open. Region band clinic and concert for the kids who made it. Area auditions for the ladder climbers. | Winter fees, region clinic fee, show entry days |
| **February** | NTCA Saturdays roll, the drumline's recycling fundraiser returns Feb 20, All-Staters go to San Antonio. | Show tickets, TMEA trip if your kid made the top |
| **March** | WGI regional season, spring break (actual family time, schedule nothing band-shaped), UIL Concert and Sight Reading prep. | Regional travel if the guard flies |
| **April** | NTCA Championships, WGI Worlds in Dayton for the top guard (April 8 to 10, 2027), UIL Concert and Sight Reading evaluation. | Dayton, if you're lucky enough to owe it |
| **May** | Spring concert, band banquet, awards, leadership and guard auditions for NEXT fall, and the show music for next season starts leaking. | Banquet tickets, flowers, tissues |
| **June** | The one real month off. Take the vacation now. This is not a suggestion. | Peace, briefly |

## The three biggest ambushes, flagged in advance

1. **November is two seasons colliding**: marching finals, winter auditions, and All-Region all stack into three weeks. Feed everyone, judge no one.
2. **Winter fees land right after fall fees end.** Ask for winter numbers in October so the November email is a confirmation, not a surprise.
3. **May auditions mean next year starts before this year ends.** The freshman year finish line is actually a lap marker. Nobody tells you that either, so we just did.
