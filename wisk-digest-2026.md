# The WISK Digest, 2026-27 Season

*The FMHS Band's weekly WISK emails, condensed and organized for quick reference. Newest week first.*

The WISK (What I Should Know) is the Flower Mound Band's official weekly email, sent through Presto Assistant on Sunday evenings. Each issue below is boiled down to actions, dates, and news. Contact emails appear exactly as the band publishes them. Passwords and discount codes are the one thing left off this public page, since it can be found by anyone on the internet. Those live in the WISK itself and in Presto.

## Week of August 17, sent Aug 16

**Actions**

- Band photos Monday, August 17. Report to the Auditorium at 5pm in football uniform, long white socks if band shoes have not arrived. Over 400 students, runs until 8pm, and there is no makeup day. If you want an individual photo, tell your kid to stay for it.
- Away Game Meal and Dietary Restriction forms due August 21, required for every student whether or not you buy meals. Kids go straight from dismissal to inspection before away games, so there is no time to eat at home.
- VOLY: LISD requires an annual background check before helping at any band activity. It takes up to two weeks and does not obligate you to anything. File it now so October-you can say yes to things.
- White marching shoes for everyone this year. Confirm or decline on the reply form, check the fit when they arrive, exchanges via shoes@fmhsband.org.
- Wind Symphony concert uniform fitting needs volunteer hands, signup in the WISK, prepping for Midwest Clinic photos.
- Drumline jackets, $100 and optional, orders close August 17 through drumline liaison Julie Riekse ("Rick-see"), personal email in the WISK.

**News**

- First Parent Preview is in the books and both marching bands are off to a strong start.
- The drumline recycling fundraiser raised $3,141 in electronics and household goods. Round two lands February 20 and the students run it.

Each week's WISK gets condensed here after it lands on Sunday. The full text lives in your email and Presto. If anything here disagrees with the WISK, the WISK wins, then open an issue so it gets fixed.
