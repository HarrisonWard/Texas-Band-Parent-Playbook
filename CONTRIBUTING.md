# Contributing

This playbook gets things wrong sometimes, and fees, rules, and dates drift every season. Corrections beat pride here. Help.

## How to help

- **Easiest**: open an Issue on this repo describing the fix ("the guard fee changed," "Region is a different Saturday," "the tea place changed its rewards program"). A link or photo as proof settles everything instantly.
- **Comfortable with GitHub**: open a pull request against the page. Small and factual merges fast.
- **Neither**: tell the maintainer in the parent groups. It'll get here.

## What belongs

- Facts with sources: dates, costs, links, rule changes, corrected names.
- Plain English. Write like you'd explain it in the pickup line.
- Year-stamps on anything that can drift ("as of fall 2026").
- FloMo specifics and general Texas knowledge both welcome, labeled as whichever they are.

## What doesn't

- Anything about specific kids. No student names, no placement gossip, no "which freshman got moved."
- Private contact info. Role emails yes, personal numbers and addresses no.
- Passwords, door codes, and discount codes. Family channels carry those.
- Drama. There is no drama section. There will never be a drama section.
- Criticism of staff, judges, or other programs. The internet has other venues.

## House style

No pep talks, no purple prose, no em dashes. Blunt and kind. Jokes dry. Sources at the bottom. If a sentence would embarrass you read aloud in a booster meeting, cut it.

## The tiebreaker

When this repo and the WISK disagree, the WISK is right and this repo has a bug. Say so, we'll fix it.

Maintained by a guard dad who learned all of this the hard way, with an AI assistant doing the typing.
