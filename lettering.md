# Lettering in Band, the One-Pager

*Yes, band kids letter. Here's how that works.*

## The short answer

A varsity letter in band is a real varsity letter, same jacket rack as football. In Texas programs, band kids typically earn it faster than parents expect, because the activity is scored, adjudicated, and time-intensive enough to satisfy any lettering standard ever written.

## The LISD system, exactly

Straight from the 2026-27 FMHS Band Handbook. Two ways to earn the letter jacket purchase:

1. **Finish junior year** while continuing into senior year, automatic.
2. **Bank 10 points in one school year**, any year, including freshman year.

**Achievement points** (reset to zero every school year):

| Achievement | Points |
|-------------|--------|
| Region band placement and event participation | 4 |
| TMEA Area advancement and audition participation | 3 |
| TMEA All-State placement and participation | 2 |
| First Division, Class 1 solo (LISD Solo and Ensemble) | 3 |
| First Division, Class 1 ensemble | 3 |
| First Division, Class 2 solo | 2 |
| First Division, Class 2 ensemble | 2 |

One solo and one ensemble count per year. Alternates only earn points if they're moved into the actual event.

**Membership points**: 2 per completed year in good standing. Freshmen start at 0, sophomores at 2, juniors at 4, stackable with achievement points.

The math that matters: a freshman who makes Region (4) and pulls First Division on a Class 1 solo (3) and ensemble (3) letters in year one. A junior enters the year with 4 banked and needs one good audition season. The ladder is climbable early, which is exactly why the directors push All-Region and Solo and Ensemble so hard.

## The jacket itself

- The earned price is about **$45 for the basic jacket and letter** at the district rate through Texas Chenille, the district's vendor. Add-ons and customization cost more and stack up over the years, budget for those gradually.
- **Fittings happen on campus in May**, directors hand the vendor the point totals, and families pay Texas Chenille directly.
- **Delivery lands in the first nine weeks of the following school year.** Letter in spring, jacket in fall. Nobody warns the kids, so warn yours.
- Order a size up. They wear hoodies under it, and ideally they wear it for decades.
- Fine print from the handbook FAQ: academic ineligibility can cost points if it blocks the event, and transfer students can earn on the same criteria.

## The part that isn't about the jacket

Watch a kid put on a letter jacket with a band patch on it and try to argue marching band isn't a sport. The jacket ends arguments at family dinners, walks into twenty-year reunions, and eventually hangs in a closet where a grandkid finds it. Worth every form it took.
